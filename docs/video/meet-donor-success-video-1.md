## Harborlight camera-readiness checklist
- No real donor PII in selected demo records.
- No Test Donor, placeholders, fake-looking values, lorem ipsum or developer labels.
- Dashboard populated and readable.
- Featured Donor 360 has giving history, engagement, relationship context and health information.
- Featured risk record has a defensible reason for status.
- Major-gift opportunity has amount, stage, owner, next action and usable Success Plan.
- Board screen has enough demo relationships/giving/engagement to tell a story.
- Funding round has realistic applicants, requested amounts, reviews and decisions.
- Community Portal is branded for Harborlight and contains no internal artifacts.
- Jarvis returns a useful donor-focused answer for the prepared prompt.
- Browser has no bookmarks bar, extensions, notifications, devtools or personal information.
- No loading/error/empty states in the intended click path.

## Capture protocol
Record each source clip for 10–20 seconds even when only 2–4 seconds will be used.

1. 2560x1440 or higher.
2. Chrome at 100% zoom unless a screen needs a deliberate lower zoom.
3. Cursor visible.
4. Notifications and bookmarks bar off.
5. Pause 1–2 seconds before and after each click.
6. Use deliberate cursor movement.
7. Capture clean takes without narration; add VO in edit.
8. Record Jarvis typing separately.

## Editing language
- Product UI is the hero; no fake holograms or generic AI imagery.
- Slow pushes and controlled crops.
- Evergreen background, teal intelligence highlights, white typography.
- Restrained UI sound cues.
- Music opens at product reveal, dips for Jarvis, builds into end card.
